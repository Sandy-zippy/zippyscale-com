Questions before you book

Why would you take the risk?

Because our systems work, and we grow when you grow. Most of our fee comes after appointments show up. So we're careful about who we take on.

What counts as qualified?

Someone in your city planning to buy or sell in the next 60 to 90 days. They answer our questions, book a time and talk to you. You'll know on the call.

Buyers or sellers?

Both. Most realtors want buyers: first-time, move-up, downsizers, investors, relocation or presale. Pick your focus in the quiz and we'll build your first guide around it.

Is my city taken?

We take one realtor per city, anywhere in North America, first come, first served. Tell us your city in the quiz. We'll confirm it on your call.

What happens on the call?

We look at your market and the guide we'd build first. Then we walk you through the payment plan and the numbers. You decide after that.

Do I keep the leads?

Yes. Every lead goes into a Google Sheet you can open anytime. They answered ads with your name on them, and nobody else in your city gets them.

How is this different from the emails I get?

You see the guide, the ads and the quiz before most of our fee is due. The leads come from your own ads, and they book time with you directly.

Do I have to make content?

No. We write the guides and run the ads. Our text, email and call reminders confirm each time. Your part is taking the calls.
