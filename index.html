Before you start

So what does it cost once it works?

$5,000 to build it, $3,000 a month to run it, and $2,500 to $20,000 when you sign a job we found, banded by contract size. The full table is above. Never a percentage of your contract, because at 3 to 8 percent net margin a percentage eats your profit. Price the success fee into a bid like any other cost of sale.

What is the catch?

You fund the ads and answer the calls we book. That is it. If you have no qualified inquiry inside 14 days, the $5,000 comes back and you keep the page, the campaigns, the answering system and the bid desk.

How much ad spend?

Sized from your scorecard answers, your sector and your area. You set the ceiling, you pay Google and Meta direct. We never touch it or take a cut.

What counts as a qualified inquiry?

A commercial project in your sector, inside your service area, at or above the minimum project size you set on the kickoff call, from an owner or tenant who has decided to build, with budget range and timeline captured. Not a price shopper, not a residential job, not someone three years out. If it does not meet that bar it does not count, and the guarantee stands.

Why not just do it free until it works?

Because free work gets treated like free work. The setup fee buys you a team that is paid to be fast, and it buys us the right to say no to firms that were never going to answer the phone. The risk is still ours: miss the 14 days and you get it all back and keep the build.

How do you know a job came from you?

The inquiry has to arrive through the system and be timestamped in your CRM, and you have to confirm the contract was signed. Your own referrals and repeat clients are never ours, even if they saw an ad on the way. If it is not in the system, we are not owed for it.

We get everything by referral. Why change?

Referrals are the best lead you get and this does not touch them. It adds the owners who never reach your network. You keep the referrals and stop being the third bid on everything else.

Which sectors and where?

Tenant improvements, clinics, restaurant and retail fit-outs, childcare, pharmacy, office, industrial and warehouse. Commercial GCs and design build firms across North America. One sector per build.
