Before you apply

The questions worth asking.

How is the 15% invoiced?

As a marketing services fee on commission you've already been paid. It isn't a commission split. No brokerage pays us, and we take no part in showing, negotiating or representing.

How do you decide a deal came from you?

The CRM stamps every enquiry on arrival and again when the desk books it. If the closed file traces to a stamp, it counts. If not, it doesn't, and you see both columns at day 90.

What counts as a held appointment?

The person shows up. No shows don't count toward the ten, and every booking carries a confirmation, a reminder and a recovery attempt.

What happens to the CRM if we stop?

You keep it. It's all built inside your accounts, in your name, from day one.

Who is actually calling my leads?

Our desk, under your name, to a script you approve. They qualify and they book. No advice on a property, no negotiating.

I already pay for leads. Why is this different?

You're buying contacts and doing the conversion yourself. Here the conversion is the product. Same ad spend, pointed at the part that leaks.

Do you work with another agent in my area?

No. One desk, one agent per market. If we're already working with someone in your postal code, we'll tell you.

Why only ten?

One desk covers about ten agents before the five minute answer time slips. When the eleventh would break the promise, we stop taking people.
