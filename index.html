What counts as a qualified project?

You set four numbers on day one: the sectors you want, your service area, your minimum project size and the timeline you can work with. A project that clears all four and books time with your estimator is qualified. You confirm it happened, and that is the trigger for payment.

Why would you work for free?

We need a documented result with a commercial builder more than we need a retainer this quarter. Three slots, then the offer closes and pricing returns to normal.

What does the AI agent actually do?

It answers every inquiry within minutes, asks what your estimator would ask, sector, square footage, location, budget range, permit stage and timeline, then scores and routes. Real projects reach a human fast. Basement renos and tire kickers go into nurture instead of onto your estimator's Friday.

We get everything by referral. Why change?

Referrals are the best lead you will ever get and this does not touch them. It adds the owners who never reach your network, the ones searching right now for a contractor who has built their exact thing. You keep the referrals and stop being the third bid on everything else.

Tenant improvements, medical, dental and veterinary clinics, restaurant and retail fit-outs, childcare, pharmacy, office build-outs, industrial and warehouse. Commercial general contractors and design build firms in Canada and the United States. We take one sector per pilot.
