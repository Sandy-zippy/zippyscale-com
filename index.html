Questions

Before you start

So what does it cost once it works?

A flat fee per signed project, banded by the size of the job. Not a percentage of your contract value, because your net margin is thin and a percentage would eat it. Not a monthly retainer you pay whether the phone rings or not. You see the whole schedule before we start, so you can price it into a bid like any other cost of sale. Think of it as a commissioned salesperson who costs nothing until they close.

What is the catch?

You fund the ad spend and answer the calls we book. That is it. If it produces nothing, we carry the build cost.

How much ad spend?

Sized from your scorecard answers, your sector and your area. You set the ceiling, you pay Google and Meta direct. We never touch it or take a cut.

What does "your first signed client" mean exactly?

A project that came through the system, walked by your estimator, and signed. You confirm it. That is the trigger for the conversation about what we get paid going forward, and not a minute before.

What do the AI agents actually do?

Answer within minutes at any hour. Ask what your estimator would ask: sector, square footage, location, budget, permit stage, timeline. Book the real ones, chase the quiet ones for weeks. A human reviews edge cases daily.

We get everything by referral. Why change?

Referrals are the best lead you get and this does not touch them. It adds the owners who never reach your network. You keep the referrals and stop being the third bid on everything else.

Which sectors and where?

Tenant improvements, clinics, restaurant and retail fit-outs, childcare, pharmacy, office, industrial and warehouse. Commercial GCs and design build firms across North America. One sector per build.
