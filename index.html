The catch, and other questions

What's the catch?

You fund your own ads. The rest of the setup is due once your first 3 qualified appointments show up. So we only take realtors we're sure we can help. We grow when you grow.

How soon do appointments start?

Your guide, ads and follow-up go live within 7 days of signing. How fast appointments come depends on your city and budget. We'll show you what's realistic on your call with us.

What about no-shows?

No-shows don't count toward your first 3 qualified appointments.

Who will I talk to?

The ZippyScale team, on a 30-minute call. We look at your market and the first guide we'd build. If you're a fit, you pick a time after the quiz.

What counts as qualified?

Someone in your city who plans to buy or sell in the next 90 days. They answered our questions and booked a time with you. You confirm it by talking to them.

Who does the work?

Our team writes your guides and runs your ads. We text and email everyone who asks for your guide. You don't make content. You see the ads before they run, and you take the calls.

Do I keep the leads?

Yes. Every lead goes into a shared sheet you can open anytime. They answered ads with your name on them. We work with one realtor per city.

Do the ads follow brokerage rules?

Your ads show your name and your brokerage. You see every ad before it runs.

Buyers or sellers?

Both. Most realtors want buyers: first-time, move-up, relocation and presale or pre-construction buyers, plus downsizers and investors. Pick your focus in the quiz and we'll build your first guide around it.
