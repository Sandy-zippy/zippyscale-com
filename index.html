Questions before you book

Who actually does the work?

Our team writes your guides and runs your ads. Follow-up by text, email and phone starts the minute someone asks for a guide. You approve the ads and take the calls.

What counts as qualified?

Someone in your city planning to buy or sell in the next 60 to 90 days. They've answered our questions and booked a time with you. You'll know on the call.

Buyers or sellers?

Both. Most realtors want buyers: first-time, move-up, downsizers, investors, relocation, presale or pre-construction. Pick your focus in the quiz and we'll build your first guide around it.

Why would you take the risk?

Because this works, and we grow when you grow. Most of our fee comes after appointments show up. So we're careful about who we take on.

Do I keep the leads?

Yes. Every lead goes into a Google Sheet you can open anytime. They answered ads with your name on them, and nobody else in your city gets them.

How is this different from the texts I get?

The people who book asked for your guide and saw your name on the ad. We do this for one realtor per city, and most of our fee comes after they show.

What happens on the call?

We look at your market and the first guide we'd build. Then we walk you through the payment plan and the numbers. You decide after that.

Is my city taken?

We take one realtor per city, anywhere in North America, first come, first served. Tell us your city in the quiz and we'll confirm it on your call.
