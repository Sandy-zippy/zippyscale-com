Before you start

The questions worth asking.

How is the 15% invoiced?

As a marketing services fee on commission you have already been paid. Not a commission split. No brokerage pays us, and we take no part in showing, negotiating or representing.

How do you decide a deal came from you?

The CRM stamps every enquiry on arrival and again when the desk books it. If the closed file traces to a stamp, it counts. If not, it does not, and you see both columns at day 90.

What happens to the CRM if we stop?

You keep it. Everything is built inside your accounts, in your name, from day one.

Who is actually calling my leads?

Our desk, under your name, to a script you approve. They qualify and they book. No advice on a property, no negotiating.

What if the appointments are junk?

An appointment counts when the person shows. No-shows do not count, and every booking carries confirmation, reminder and recovery.

I already pay for leads. Why is this different?

You are buying contacts and doing the conversion yourself. Here the conversion is the product. Same ad spend, pointed at the part that leaks.

Do you work with another agent in my area?

No. One desk, one agent per market.

How fast does it start?

Campaigns live in seven days. The desk answers from the day the first ad runs.
