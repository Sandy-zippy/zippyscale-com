Questions

Before you take a slot

So what does it cost once it works?

A flat fee per signed project, banded by the size of the job. Not a percentage of your contract value, because your net margin is thin and a percentage would eat it. Not a monthly retainer you pay whether the phone rings or not. You see the whole schedule before we start, so you can price it into a bid like any other cost of sale. Think of it as a commissioned salesperson who costs nothing until they close.

What is the catch?

You fund the ad spend and you commit to answering the calls we book. That is it. We carry the build. If it does not produce a signed project, we carry that cost too.

How much ad spend?

We size it from your scorecard answers, your sector and your area, and you set the ceiling. It is paid direct to Google and Meta on your own accounts. We never touch it and we never take a cut of it.

What does "your first signed client" mean exactly?

A project that came through the system, walked by your estimator, and signed. You confirm it. That is the trigger for the conversation about what we get paid going forward, and not a minute before.

What do the AI agents actually do?

Four jobs. Answer every inquiry within minutes at any hour. Ask what your estimator would ask, sector, square footage, location, budget range, permit stage and timeline. Book the qualified ones into the calendar. Chase the quiet ones for weeks without getting bored. A human on our side reviews the edge cases every day.

We get everything by referral. Why change?

Referrals are the best lead you will ever get and this does not touch them. It adds the owners who never reach your network, the ones searching right now for a contractor who has built their exact thing. You keep the referrals and stop being the third bid on everything else.
